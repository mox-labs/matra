# Field notes

The survey team reached the northern ridge on the third day. Snow had closed the lower pass, so the route ran along the eastern face instead.

> Radio contact was lost for six hours.

## Findings

Three of the five markers were intact and matched their recorded positions. The fourth had been moved about ten metres downhill by a rockfall, and its base plate was cracked. The fifth could not be found, although the team searched the slope below it for most of an afternoon. A replacement marker will be set on the next visit, once the pass is open.
