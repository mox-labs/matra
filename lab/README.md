# Lab

The Lab is where matra's evals and experiments will live: measurements of
how well matra does what it claims, and trials of what it might do next,
each with the data and the method that produced it. Nothing is here yet.

The Lab sits apart from the documentation on purpose. The pages under Docs
describe what ships, and the records under Blueprints say what was decided
and why. An experiment is neither: it can fail, and a result it reports is
evidence for a decision rather than the decision itself. When an
experiment's result changes what matra does, the change goes through the
process in [Blueprints](../blueprints/README.md), and the experiment is
cited there.
